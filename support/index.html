PromptTrail Support

简体中文版 ↓

PromptTrail is a local-first library for AI workflows: split long prompts into steps, keep the original prompt and a reusable template side by side, and import, favorite, and version your work.

How do I import a workflow?

Copy JSON in the prompttrail.workflow format, open the Workflows tab, tap Import → "Read from Clipboard" → preview the steps → "Confirm Import".

Is my data uploaded?

No. Everything stays on your device. The app has no backend, no networking, and no analytics; deleting the app deletes your data.

How do I edit a very long step?

Open a workflow → Edit. The list shows summaries only; open a step and the original prompt and template scroll inside fixed-height editors, with a find bar to jump straight to the text you need.

How do I buy PromptTrail Pro, and restore a purchase?

Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple. You can restore it on any device signed in with the same Apple ID. Refunds are handled by Apple according to their standard policy.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone, iPad, Mac (separate Mac app), and foldables. On iPad and Mac the step list and editor appear side by side.

I deleted a step by mistake.

Use Version History to review and restore a previous version.

PromptTrail 支持

English ↑

PromptTrail 是一款本地优先的 AI 工作流提示词库：把长提示词拆成步骤，保留原文与可复用模板，支持导入、收藏与版本记录。

如何导入工作流？

复制符合 prompttrail.workflow 格式的 JSON，进入「工作流」页点击导入 →「从剪贴板读取」→ 预览步骤 →「确定导入」。

我的数据会上传吗？

不会。所有内容只保存在本机，App 无后端、不联网、不统计；卸载 App 即删除数据。

长步骤怎么编辑？

打开工作流 → 编辑，列表只显示摘要；点进某一步后，原文与模板在固定高度编辑框内滚动，可用查找栏直接定位要改的文字。

如何购买 PromptTrail Pro？如何恢复购买？

Pro 为一次性买断，购买与退款完全由 Apple 处理；可在任意登录同一 Apple ID 的设备上恢复购买。

支持哪些设备？

iPhone、iPad、Mac（Mac 版为独立 App）与折叠屏；iPad / Mac 上步骤列表与编辑区同屏显示。

误删了步骤怎么办？

在版本记录中可回看并恢复历史版本。
